# CaptureProof

> CaptureProof is a HIPAA-compliant visual health record and telemedicine platform for secure photo and video communication between patients and clinicians. Its "Smart Medical Camera" apps let providers and patients capture consistent medical-quality imagery, organize and compare it chronologically, and layer AI-driven visual biometrics and clinical insight on top for remote patient monitoring and asynchronous care.
